02 · Reasoned redesigns

Four businesses, re-argued

Each began by measuring what existed — contrast, hierarchy, weight, story — and ended in a full redesign where every decision carries its why. Each case below publishes that reasoning in full.

3

“Measured” is literal: before proposing anything I measure what exists — contrast ratios against the background actually painted, layout at 320 px, page weight, heading structure. The redesign then has to defend itself against those numbers, not against taste. And where a concept is unsolicited, it says so on its face: those were prepared from public materials, delivered privately with a standing take-down offer, never published as the brand's own.

04 · How I work

Embedded, end to end

You bring the work you've sold; I produce it — design and build — under your flag or mine. Inside your team: your channels, your standups, your deadlines — remote, on US-aligned hours.

5

Under whose flag: white-label production for your clients, or credited work under mine — agreed in writing. What never changes: the reasoning ships with the piece, so the person defending the work in the room is never defending it empty-handed.

  1. Diagnose. Measure the current state — numbers, not impressions.
  2. Propose. The concept arrives with its written argument.
  3. Build. Hand-authored, no framework bloat.
  4. Verify. Accessibility measured, 320 px proven, weight counted.

Handoffs arrive complete — design, build, and the written reasoning — so nothing needs a second meeting to be understood.

This site holds itself to the same standard.

05 · The principal

One person, on purpose

I'm Carlos Marruffo — systems engineer and this studio's one member. I've spent two decades shipping software: I founded three companies and built one of them into an immersive-tech studio whose work served Hewlett-Packard, Cisco and RSA — until it was acquired in 2022.

Since then I've worked inside a Chicago SaaS as project manager and then product designer — running a distributed team, owning research, design and the full 2026 product roadmap end to end. That is the shape of work I do best: one product, one team, everything from the diagnosis to the shipped build.

I work in English and Spanish, from Lima, on US-aligned hours. This site is the method in practice: every decision on it is measured, and every reason is written down.
