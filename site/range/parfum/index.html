01 · The accord

A perfume is three decisions.

Everything you smell arrives in order. What greets you, what stays, and what is still there tomorrow on a scarf. The three bars beside every bottle on this site are that order, drawn.

Top · first 15 minutes

What greets you

Citrus, herbs, anything volatile. It is the handshake, and it is meant to leave. A fragrance still smelling of its opening at hour four was built to impress a shop, not a person.

Heart · hours 1 to 4

What it actually is

Florals, spice, the accord the composition is named for. This is the part a house is judged on, and the part that costs money. It is where our budget goes.

Base · hour 4 onward

What stays

Amber, vanilla, woods, tobacco. Heavy molecules that hold on to fabric. If a scent disappears by lunch, this is the part someone decided to save on.

Read the bars, not the box. Every bottle on this page carries the same three marks in the same order, so you can compare two fragrances without reading a word of copy. It is the only spec sheet perfumery has, and most shops hide it behind an adjective.

02 · The house

Inspired-by is usually made wherever it is cheapest. Ours is made in France.

An interpretation is a legitimate craft with a long history: a perfumer reads a composition and rebuilds it from raw materials. What makes it worth buying is who rebuilds it, and out of what. Ours are composed on this bench, from materials bought by the kilo in the same valley the originals buy from, and bottled in the same country.

We are not going to pretend that is a secret formula. It is just the expensive way of doing it, kept up for forty-six years.

07 · Straight answers

The questions people actually send us.

Is this a copy of another perfume?

It is an interpretation, which is a different thing and an old one. A perfumer reads a well-known composition and rebuilds it from raw materials, making their own choices about proportion and quality. We do not use another house's name, packaging or formula, and we do not claim to be them.

Why is it $19.99 when the original is $150?

Because most of that $150 is not the liquid. It is the licence, the campaign, the celebrity, the department store margin and the weight of the glass. We carry the composition and the manufacturing, and we sell direct. The concentrate percentage is printed on every page.

Where is it actually made?

Composed, macerated and filled in France, in Grasse. That is on the base of every bottle, and it is the single fastest way to tell a real house from a relabelled one: ask where it was filled, not where it was shipped from.

How long does it last on skin?

The eaux de toilette hold four to six hours; the Prestige eaux de parfum, at 20 percent concentrate, hold six to eight. Skin type moves that more than anything we can do. Fabric holds all of them much longer.

Can I return it once it is opened?

Yes, within thirty days, opened, no explanation required. A fragrance you cannot try is a fragrance you cannot buy honestly, so the risk sits with us rather than with you.

Do you test on animals?

No. Not by us, not by a laboratory acting for us, and not in any market that would require it as a condition of sale.
