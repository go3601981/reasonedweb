01 · The product as it is

A real system, stated precisely

Sound Suite is a shipping LG product, announced at CES 2026 — the first soundbar system with Dolby Atmos FlexConnect. The system this demonstration depicts: the H7 soundbar carrying the front stage and dialogue, M7 and M5 wireless satellites for surround, and the W7 wireless subwoofer — scaling up to 9.1.6 in premium configurations, driven by LG's Alpha 11 processor.

02 · The correction that shaped the concept

Nothing moves. That's the point.

The first proof of concept animated the speakers — they turned to follow the listener across the room. It looked wonderful, and it was wrong: Sound Suite's speakers are fixed wireless units. Nothing physically moves.

What the product actually does is invisible. Sound Follow tracks the listener's position and re-centers the sweet spot by re-balancing per-speaker levels and timing — what you hear is a blend across the room, re-tuned around you. So the second version removed the false metaphor and built the honest one: you hear the room re-balance as you move, instead of watching hardware perform something it doesn't do.

An experience that misrepresents the product would not survive its first meeting with the client's own engineers. The correction cost a striking visual; it bought the demonstration its credibility.

2

From the project's product-truth document: “Speakers DO NOT physically move or rotate. They are fixed wireless units.” The demo's job is to make an invisible feature audible — not to invent a visible one. Two dramatizations remain, and they are declared in note [3].

03 · Implementation

From CAD data to a published build

  • 3D models derived from manufacturer CAD data in the course of the engagement
  • Binaural audio, so headphones stand in for the five speakers
  • A five-chapter interactive experience, embeddable as a static route
  • Bilingual delivery: English and Canadian French
  • Installation and user manuals, bilingual
  • WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility review (June 2026)

Where the demonstration dramatizes, it says so.

3

Declared liberties: assigning a specific instrument to a specific corner of the room is the demo's creative choice, not product behavior — the real system renders a blend, not one clean channel per box. And the re-balance glide is deliberately slowed so you can hear it happen; the real system settles faster. A demonstration earns trust by labeling its dramatizations, not by hiding them.
