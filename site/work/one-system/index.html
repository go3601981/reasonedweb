Case study

One system, forty-six verticals

Forty-six complete sites for forty-six businesses that do not exist, built on one production system for the Peruvian market — in Spanish, WhatsApp-first, each one argued from its own trade.

Speculative concepts — fictional businesses, built as demonstrations for the Peruvian market
01 · The premise

Volume is easy. Volume without decay is the argument.

Anyone can produce forty-six pages. The question a production partner actually gets hired on is whether the forty-sixth holds the standard of the first. This corpus is that test, run in public: one structural system underneath — offer stated in the hero, services, proof, contact — and on top of it, every vertical re-argued from its own trade. The cebichería and the law practice share a skeleton and nothing you can see.

1

Honesty first, as everywhere on this site: all forty-six businesses are invented. Names, addresses, reviews and phone numbers are demonstration content — the label at the top of this page is about the businesses depicted, and it applies to every pixel below. The pieces themselves were built as complete, shippable pages: full content below every fold, not covers.

02 · The wall

Forty-six heroes, one discipline

Every tile below is the opening viewport of a finished page — photographed from the running piece, not composed in a design tool. Read it at squint distance: the repetition is the point. What repeats is the completeness; what never repeats is the face.

2

Method: each piece was loaded in a real browser at 1440 px and its first viewport captured — forty-six captures, taken with the same tooling this site uses to verify itself. Nothing was staged for the wall; these are the pages as they run.

03 · What varies, what never does

The system is invisible on purpose

What changes with every vertical: the palette, argued from the trade itself — sea and ají for the cebichería, leather and serif restraint for the law firm, clinical calm for the veterinaria. The photography direction. The voice: a barbershop does not speak like a wealth-management office. What never changes: the offer stated in the first viewport, services a customer can actually buy, proof before the ask, and contact through the channel that market genuinely uses — WhatsApp, one tap away.

3

WhatsApp-first is a market decision, not a preference: for Peruvian small business, the inbox is WhatsApp. A system that ignores how its market actually buys is a portfolio piece; one that respects it is production work. The same reasoning that puts a form and an email address on this site puts a green button on those.

04 · Spanish is a feature

The same discipline, in two languages

This corpus is in Spanish because its market is. The studio works in English and Spanish natively — the cases on this site ship in US English, the LG engagement delivered in English and Canadian French, and these forty-six argue in the Spanish of the businesses they represent. For an agency, that is not trivia: it is LATAM-market capability and US-hours production in the same person.
