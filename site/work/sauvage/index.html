Eau de Parfum — Concept Study

SAUVAGE

An ode to wide-open spaces
Scroll
01 — Magic Hour

There is an hour when
the desert holds its breath

When light turns blue and the horizon burns its last ember. When everything wild becomes noble. Sauvage is born in that hour — raw freshness, power and nobility in a single breath.

02 — Top Note

Calabrian
Bergamot

A burst of raw freshness, sparkling like first light on cold stone. Vivid, bright, immediate — the opening line of the composition.

03 — Heart Note

Sichuan
Pepper

An electric spice, vibrant and magnetic. It doesn't burn — it hums, wrapping the freshness in warmth and tension.

04 — Base Note

Ambroxan

A mineral, woody trail drawn from rare ambergris notes. It lingers the way dusk lingers — long after the sun has gone.

05 — The Bottle

Deep blue,
almost black

A gradient carved into glass, sealed by a magnetic clasp. The bottle holds the night the way the desert holds the heat — quietly, and for a long time.

06 — The Collection

One name,
four intensities

Eau de Toilette

Fresh · Citrus · Woody

Eau de Parfum

Citrus · Vanilla

Parfum

Woody · Ambery

Elixir

Spicy · Fresh · Woody

Born in nature,
shaped by hand

The trail ends where the desert begins again.
