Instructions

Three steps, and the first one is the one people skip.

  • Start on clean, dry skin. No moisturiser, no oil, nothing underneath. Product left on the skin is the usual reason a strip will not stay put.
  • Press the line flat, then apply. Smooth the wrinkle out with a fingertip but do not stretch the skin, then lay the patch over the flattened area.
  • Wear up to ten hours, then peel slowly. Take it off gently rather than quickly. Some strips can be reused once or twice.

Questions

The four we are asked most.

How long can I wear Smoothies?

Up to 10 hours in any 24 hour period, day or night. For best results wear them 4 to 5 times a week for 30 days, and after that as needed.

Are Smoothies reusable?

Some strips can be reused once or twice. If an edge leaves a mark on the skin, applying a used strip over that mark is the fix we recommend.

Can I use retinol with Smoothies?

It is not recommended. How long to pause retinol before using them varies from person to person, so test first and ask a dermatologist if you are unsure.

Can I use Smoothies after botox?

Yes. The patches are mechanical: they hold the skin and do nothing to the muscle, so they sit alongside whatever else you are doing.
