The Woods & Co — proof in one continuous story

I built the current production website for The Woods & Co, an independent lifestyle communications firm. The site turns a deep client record into a browsable story across beauty, wellness, fitness, home, and hospitality.

01 — Information architecture

One page, ten purposeful stops

The navigation names the whole argument before the reader starts: positioning, clients, work, lifestyle, story, services, advisors, leadership, testimonials, and contact. Each stop answers a different question instead of repeating the same proof in a new format.

1

This is an information-architecture claim, not a business-result claim. It describes what is present on the production site and how the interface orders it.

02 — Work as evidence

A portfolio that can be interrogated

The logo tracks establish range; the work section carries the detail. Readers can filter the portfolio by beauty, wellness, fitness, or lifestyle, then open individual case studies instead of treating every engagement as an undifferentiated tile.

Every card pairs the client with the kind of engagement and a concise statement of the published evidence. That makes the page useful to someone scanning quickly while preserving a route to the fuller record.

2

Campaign outcomes and client testimonials on the live site are The Woods & Co's published record. This case documents the website build; it does not present those outcomes as results of the web work.

03 — Interaction

Motion with an off switch

The film, four client-logo tracks, and testimonial sequence all expose direct controls. A visitor can start the film, pause the moving logo rows, move through testimonials one at a time, or pause them entirely.

The Instagram feed waits behind an explicit load button. The page explains that loading it connects to Instagram and keeps a normal profile link available if the embedded feed is unavailable.

04 — Editorial rhythm

Long-form without dead ends

The page alternates concise positioning, dense proof, profiles, and expandable service detail rather than forcing every kind of content into one card pattern. The numbered navigation remains a map of the whole page, while case-study links and the inquiry path create clear exits when the reader is ready.

3

The build keeps a single page from becoming a single path. Anchor navigation, filter controls, expandable disclosures, and linked case studies let the reader choose the depth that fits the question they arrived with.
