04Meet Susan Woods

The relationships behind the placements.

Principal Susan Woods has over 18 years of experience marketing consumer lifestyle, health, fitness and beauty products — and the people behind them. Prior to launching The Woods & Co she co-founded Crier Communications, a bicoastal PR firm with an expertise in food and health; under her initiative, Crier branched into fitness and beauty and opened a New York City office.

She holds a degree in Art History and Journalism from New York University, was awarded membership in Kappa Tau Alpha, the national journalism honor society, and worked in marketing for the Whitney Museum and the Museum of Modern Art.
