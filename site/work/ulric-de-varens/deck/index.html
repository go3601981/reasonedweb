Global presence

Our location

Ulric de Varens' headquarters is located in Paris, France, near the Avenue des Champs-Elysées. 100% independent French fragrance house. The company's logistics center is based in Bernay, Normandy, and serves France as well as other regions worldwide.

We have a global presence, operating in over 90 countries, with six subsidiaries located in USA, Germany, Turkey, Romania, Greece, and Dubai.
